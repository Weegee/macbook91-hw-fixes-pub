macbook91-hw-fixes 20261026.01-1 - RELEASED (tag v20261026.01, 2026-10-26; released without the hardware gate, which is NOT_RUN)

What: kernel series. The LTS kernel is the package's default, and a pacman
hook reports every installed kernel the two DKMS modules do not cover. The
module sources and the udev rule are unchanged.
- New /usr/lib/macbook91-hw-fixes/kernel-series and the pacman hook
  71-macbook91-hw-fixes-series.hook (after 70-dkms-install.hook, on kernel
  installs and upgrades and on this package): a WARNING block for each
  installed kernel of a series without an applesmc source (no charge limit of
  this package there, boot the LTS kernel), one note line for a series
  macbook91_backlight is not tested on (new kernel/TESTED-SERIES: 6.18, 7.2).
  It builds nothing and never stops the transaction. The install scriptlet
  and macbook91-hw-verify ask the same script.
- macbook91-hw-verify on such a kernel names the kernel's own SMC driver
  (macsmc-acpi, applesmc or none) instead of claiming that in-tree applesmc
  runs, reports a charge_control_end_threshold of another power supply as
  INFO, and reports an untested backlight series as INFO.
- depends: linux-cachyos-lts-headers (pulls in linux-cachyos-lts) and
  findutils; optdepends: linux-cachyos-headers. All inside package().
- No 7.3 series for macbook91-applesmc: CachyOS 7.3 builds no applesmc and
  brings its own SMC driver macsmc-acpi, which uses the SMC ports without
  reserving them; a 7.3 applesmc would load next to it by its DMI alias
  (docs/FINDINGS.md). The patch applies to v7.3 with fuzz 0 and builds
  against the 7.3.0-4 headers; that is not the obstacle. The series 6.18 and
  7.2 stay. Open, for the maintainer: rely on the kernel's macsmc on 7.3
  after a check on the hardware, or build applesmc for 7.3 and keep
  macsmc-acpi from loading.
- Details: docs/CHANGELOG.md.

Source tarball against that of 20261021.01: the module sources, their

rule are unchanged (the packaged DKMS trees differ from the installed
